## Research backlog and acceptance

Before P7 approval, retrieve the applicable Income Tax Ordinance and amendments, Finance Act 2026, relevant rules/notifications and explanatory material. Capture legal section, effective period, source publication date, retrieval date and hash of public source bytes when available. Resolve conflicting text; do not treat search snippets as legislation.

Build an applicability matrix for salary, bank profit, investments/gains, property income/disposals, gifts/remittances, personal expenses, liabilities and withholding. Include foreign exposure or business only if applicable and researched. Validate rates, thresholds, rounding, exemptions, credit rules, tax-paid presentation, asset valuation basis and wealth bridge treatment with worked cases. Every implemented rule needs authoritative support and tests; unknown treatment blocks approval.

Revalidate at year close and immediately before filing for amendments, deadlines, form revisions and revised-return procedures. Historical examples may inform questions but cannot certify the current rules. No filing deadline or numeric rate is promised by this plan.
